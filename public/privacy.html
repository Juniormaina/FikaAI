Privacy notice

Last updated: 25 November 2026

FikaAI gives farm advisory guidance for crops and poultry. This notice explains what the app stores, where, and the choices you have. It is written in plain language; Hii ni taarifa ya faragha kwa lugha rahisi.

What we handle

  • What you type: crop or animal problems, your region, and any sale you record (item, quantity, amount).
  • Your saved field plans and your language choice (English or Kiswahili).
  • Channel details: for SMS or USSD, the identifier of the session or phone line so replies reach you and menus remember where you are.

We do not ask for your national ID, bank or mobile-money details, or precise GPS location. Please do not type them.

Where it is kept

  • On your device: saved plans and queued questions are kept in your browser (IndexedDB) so the app works offline. Clearing site data removes them.
  • On the server: when the backend is running, messages, recorded sales, plans and USSD menu state are stored in its database so the web, SMS and USSD channels agree with each other.
  • The static web version has no server and sends nothing anywhere.

Why

Only to answer your questions, remember your plans and sales, and keep the channels consistent. We do not sell your data and do not use it for advertising.

Optional AI model

If the operator turns on an AI model, the text of your question may be sent to that model provider to draft an answer. Without it, answers come from a built-in keyword matcher and the advisory library on the device or server.

Your choices

  • Delete saved plans in the app or clear your browser's site data.
  • Ask the operator to access, correct or delete the server records tied to you, under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.
  • Stop using SMS or USSD at any time; operator and network charges are set by your carrier.

Safety note

Guidance is decision support only and does not replace a certified agronomist or veterinarian. For sudden deaths in a flock, severe symptoms or pesticide poisoning, contact an expert straight away.
