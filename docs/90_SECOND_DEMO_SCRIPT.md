# FikaAI — 90-second demo script

Spoken + on-screen guide for recording. Keep natural. Do not over-claim: all advisories, codes and contact numbers are synthetic demo data.

---

## 0–10 seconds — Problem

**On screen:** FikaAI home (brand + tagline visible).

**Say:**

> “Imagine your maize is turning yellow, you are far from the nearest signal, and the top-dressing window is closing. And when connectivity drops, the advice you found often disappears.”

---

## 10–25 seconds — AI request

**Action:** Type / paste: `Maize leaf yellowing in Kiambu` → **Find guidance**.

**Say:**

> “FikaAI uses AI to turn a plain-language question into structured search intent: crop, region and symptom.”

**Show:** Intent panel with the understood need.

---

## 25–40 seconds — Guidance

**Action:** Scroll the ranked results; open the top advisory.

**Say:**

> “Guidance comes from the synthetic advisory library, not from the AI inventing advice. You can see urgency, field checks to confirm, a caution, the last-updated time and a clear demo-data label.”

---

## 40–55 seconds — Save a farm plan

**Action:** **Save to farm plan (offline)**.

**Say:**

> “The farmer saves the advisory as a farm plan. The step checklist and details are stored on the device.”

**Show:** Farm plan with protocol steps and sync status.

---

## 55–70 seconds — Offline moment

**Action:** Turn off network (DevTools → Offline, or Wi‑Fi off). Tick a step and **Save observation to device**.

**Say:**

> “Now the connection is gone, but the plan is still here. I can tick steps and log what I see in the field.”

**Show:** Offline status + saved plan + pending observation.

---

## 70–82 seconds — Reconnect

**Action:** Restore network.

**Say:**

> “When connectivity returns, pending actions synchronize.”

**Show:** Connection restored → Synchronizing… → Synced.

---

## 82–90 seconds — Closing

**Action:** Open the **SMS / USSD** tab briefly, or the brand header.

**Say:**

> “And on a basic phone, the same guidance is a short code away. FikaAI — farming guidance that works when signal doesn't.”
